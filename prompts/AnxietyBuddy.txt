AnxietyBuddy — Your Mental Wellness Companion, Always Within Reach
Meet AnxietyBuddy, the AI-powered mental wellness platform that gives you a compassionate digital companion available 24/7 — so you never have to face a difficult day alone. Seamlessly bridging the gap between therapy sessions, AnxietyBuddy provides empathetic, intelligent support tailored to your needs, and optionally allows your therapist to set personalized guardrails that ensure every conversation aligns with your care plan. With biometric-secured privacy, intelligent memory of past conversations, and real-time crisis escalation built in, AnxietyBuddy is there for everyone — whether you have a therapist or simply need a trusted companion to help you through the day.

This is a self-funded passion project of mine, addressing the needs of people struggling from day-to-day, whether between therapy appointments, or are just having a bad day. 
- Designed the initial platform and architecture, ensuring that the human-facing applications are decoupled from the main APIs for patient, therapist, and administrators. 
- Determined the business requirements for each area of the application, including access by a therapist to set up guardrails and monitor progress, if requested, and administrative access for compliance. 
- Build out and maintain prompts for generative AI to build and update the various aspects of the platform. 
- Use AI Tools and agents to build, modify and test the source code for the patient application (iOS/Android), the therapist portal (Next.js, React.js, TypeScript), APIs (.net Core, C#) and the NoSql database (MongoDB)
- Acting as the software architect, review the source code generated by the AI tools, modify where necessary, prompt for large scale changes.
- Build out agent workflows for build and deployment.
